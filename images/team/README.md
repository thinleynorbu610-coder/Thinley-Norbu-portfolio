# Team photos

Every Nexora member shows a profile icon until a real photo is added.

To add one:
1. Put the photo here, e.g. `tshewang-dorji.jpg` (a portrait works best, at least 300px wide).
2. Open [js/data.js](../../js/data.js), find that person under `team.members`, and change
   `photo:null` to `photo:"images/team/tshewang-dorji.jpg"`.
